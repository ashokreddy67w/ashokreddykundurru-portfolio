Every product starts with a problem someone is tired of. For EMAS Attendance, it was attendance tracked in spreadsheets and chat messages.

## Start small

The first version did one thing: let employees check in and out, and let a manager see it. Everything else came after real feedback.

## What I learned

- Talk to the people who have the problem before writing code.
- Security and data isolation are product features, not afterthoughts.
- One codebase for web and mobile kept a small team fast.
